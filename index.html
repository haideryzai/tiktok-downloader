Privacy Policy

TikTok Video Downloader

Last Updated: April 4, 2026

Thank you for using TikTok Video Downloader. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Local Storage Only: Our app stores all data locally on your device. We do not have access to your personal information, download history, or downloaded content.

  • Download History: Stored locally in a database on your device (video titles, URLs, download dates)
  • Downloaded Content: Videos and thumbnails are saved to your device's storage
  • App Settings: Preferences like theme mode and download quality are stored locally

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To allow you to download and manage TikTok videos
  • To display your download history within the app
  • To save your preferences and settings
  • To provide notifications when downloads complete

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • TikTok API (via tikwm.com): Used to fetch video information and download URLs. Your TikTok video URL is sent to this service to retrieve video data.
  • yt-dlp: A video downloading library used to download TikTok videos to your device.
Note: When you paste a TikTok link to download, the URL is processed through a third-party service. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. Please review their privacy policies before use.

4. Permissions Required

Our app requires the following permissions to function:

  • Storage Access: To save downloaded videos to your device
  • Network Access: To fetch video information and download content
  • Notifications: To notify you when downloads complete

5. Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete:

  • Individual downloads from the app's history
  • All download history by clearing app data
  • Downloaded videos from your device's Downloads folder

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

You have the right to:
